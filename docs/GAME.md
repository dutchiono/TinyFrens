# Game &amp; RPG

TinyFrens blends tamagotchi care with social play. The core is simple now; more RPG systems are planned.

## Core gameplay

- **Care loop**: Feed, Play, Nap, Clean, Vitamin. Each action affects mood/energy/needs.
- **Mood &amp; energy**: Your fren reacts based on care. Neglect makes them grumpy/tired; good care keeps them happy.
- **Growth &amp; personality**: Frens show different poses/moods; behavior reflects how you care for them.
- **Your NFT**: The companion is your owned NFT. Ownership is rechecked every 24 hours while in use.
- **Device-local saves**: Care state and preferences stay on your device (with linked accounts syncing settings across devices).

## Social features

- **Visits**: Drop in on other frens when connected. See their companions in context.
- **Party rooms**: Shared spaces to hang out with frens together ([Party rooms &amp; visits](party-rooms.md)).
- **Live presence**: See who's around in supported contexts (web/Telegram).
- **Collection pride**: Hood Turtles, Robots v1, Nubs, Bots Computers all represented.

## Planned RPG direction

- **Quests &amp; adventures**: Bite-sized quests, daily/weekly goals, collection-specific stories.
- **Items &amp; toys**: Collect/use items that change mood, unlock poses, or give boosts.
- **Abilities &amp; traits**: Frens may gain quirks/abilities tied to collection or care history.
- **Worlds**: Different scenes/rooms tied to collections (Hood Turtles experience, Tamagotchi Robots vibe).
- **Economy interplay**: FRENS token ties into on-chain economy ([ONCHAIN-ECONOMY.md](../ONCHAIN-ECONOMY.md)) with potential future gameplay uses.
- **Progression**: Long-term care history could unlock cosmetic/story rewards.

## Collections flavor

- **Robots v1**: Futuristic, playful robot frens.
- **Hood Turtles**: Turtle crew with street/hood vibes ([Hood Turtles experience](hood-turtles-experience.md)).
- **Tamagotchi Robots**: Robot tamagotchi aesthetic ([Tamagotchi Robots](tamagotchi-robots.md)).
- **Nubs / Bots Computers**: Distinct personalities and art.

The RPG layer is intentionally additive — keep the tamagotchi feel while expanding quests/social without losing accessibility.
