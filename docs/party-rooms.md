# Party rooms & visits 🎉

TinyFrens isn't just a solo pet — you can see other people's frens hanging out with
yours. There are **two different ways** to be "together," and they work differently on
purpose. This guide explains both.

> **Short version:** *Party rooms* = a shared space where many frens appear together,
> no permission needed. *Visits* = one friend's fren comes to see yours, and you have to
> say yes first.

---

## Party rooms

A **party room** is a shared space. Everyone in the same room sees everyone else's fren
drawn together in one scene. Think of it like a shared room you can all walk into.

Open **◌ Party** in the app to pick a room.

### The three kinds of rooms

| Room | How you join | Who's there |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Tap **Join public room** | Everyone, everywhere, who also chose the public room |
| **Private** | Enter a **room code** (3–24 characters, letters/numbers) | Only people who know that exact code |
| **Chat room** | Opened from a **Telegram group** or **Discord channel** | Everyone in that specific group/channel |

- **Private codes** are great for friends, a class, or a community — share the code and
  you're all in the same room. There's no friend request or approval; the code *is* the
  key.
- **Chat rooms** are the magic of Telegram/Discord: add the bot to a group, tap **Open the
  room**, and that whole group becomes one shared room automatically. See
  [Playing in Telegram](telegram.md).

### What you see

Each room shows every member's fren with its **name, species, and colour**, gently
bobbing around the scene. When someone changes their fren's look, it updates live for
everyone in the room.

- Rooms hold up to **80 frens** at once.
- **Leaving** is one tap — your fren instantly disappears from the shared scene.
- Party rooms are **cosmetic**. Being in a room never changes your fren's food, joy, or
  energy, and other people can't touch your pet.

### Good to know

- Party rooms need the **TinyFrens server** to be reachable (they're how multiple people
  sync). Your own fren and its care always work even if the server is down — you just
  can't see the shared room until it reconnects.
- You're only ever identified by your fren's display name and look. No location, photos,
  or private messages are shared in a room.

## Visits (a friend comes to see your fren)

A **visit** is more personal than a party room. One specific friend's fren drops in to
visit **your** fren, and **you have to accept first**.

### How a visit works

1. You **send a visit invite** to someone you've added as a friend.
2. They get a request and choose **Let them visit** or **Not now**.
3. If they accept, your friend's fren appears next to yours for **up to 30 minutes**.
4. Either of you can **say goodbye** to end it early. It also ends automatically when the
  time is up or if someone closes the app.

### The rules that keep it safe

- **Nothing is automatic.** A visit only happens after the other person accepts. You can
  always decline.
- **Your fren stays yours.** A visiting fren is just a guest — it never replaces your pet,
  and it can't change your saved food, joy, energy, name, or look.
- **Visitors are a snapshot.** They see your fren's current name and appearance. Core
  care (Feed/Play/Nap) is always done by *you*, the owner.
- **It expires.** Invites and visits are temporary by design — nothing lingers.

### Party room vs. visit — at a glance

| | Party room | Visit |
|---|---|---|
| How many people | Up to 80 | Just you + one friend |
| Permission needed | No (code or shared chat) | **Yes** — the host accepts |
| Where it shows | In the **◌ Party** tab | Beside your pet on the **♡ Pet** tab |
| How long | As long as you stay | Up to 30 minutes |
| Changes your pet? | Never | Never |

## Friends vs. rooms vs. visits

These three get mixed up, so here's the clean distinction:

- **Friends** — a saved, mutual connection (you add each other by code and both accept).
  Needed before you can send someone a **visit**.
- **Party rooms** — an open shared space. No friendship required; a code or a shared chat
  is enough.
- **Visits** — a consented, temporary one-on-one guest appearance for **friends**.

You don't need friends to enjoy party rooms, and you don't need a room to receive a visit.

## FAQ

**Do I need friends to join a room?**
No. Public and private rooms only need the server (and a code for private ones). Friends
are only for visits.

**Can someone in a room feed or rename my fren?**
No. Rooms are view-only for other people. Only you can care for or restyle your own fren.

**My room shows "Connecting to the TinyFrens server…"**
That's normal if the server is briefly unreachable. Your fren and its care still work;
the shared scene reconnects on its own. Try again in a few seconds.

**Is my private room code a secret?**
Treat it like one. Anyone with the code can join that room. Make up a fresh code if you
want a fresh space.

**Where do visits show up?**
On your **♡ Pet** tab, next to your own fren — not in the party room.
