# FAQ

## Gameplay

**Q: What do the care actions do?**
A: Feed (food), Play (happiness), Nap (energy), Clean/Vitamin as needed. Mood/energy reflect care over time.

**Q: Can I visit other frens?**
A: Yes — visits let you drop in on others; party rooms let groups hang out ([Party rooms](party-rooms.md)).

**Q: What collections are supported?**
A: Hood Turtles, Robots v1, The Nubs, Bots Computers. More may come.

**Q: Is there an RPG/quests?**
A: Core tamagotchi is live. Quests/adventures/items are planned — see [Game &amp; RPG](GAME.md).

## Wallet &amp; ownership

**Q: Do you take my NFTs?**
A: No. Only read-only signatures. No transfers, approvals, or gas.

**Q: What if I sell my NFT?**
A: Ownership checked on connect, renewed every 24 hours. After expiry, display stops until reverified (needs internet). Unlinking removes access immediately.

**Q: How does Bankr pairing work?**
A: App gives temporary pairing code → share with Bankr → Bankr signs read-only message → approve on that device. Device credential never enters chat.

## Devices

**Q: Can iPhone float over other apps?**
A: Not yet. iPhone runs inside TinyFrens (home-screen app). Android and desktop support floating overlays. Native iOS App Store app in prep.

**Q: Android overlay not working?**
A: Grant "Display over other apps", then show pet again.

**Q: Does it work on desktop?**
A: Yes — Windows/Mac floating pet builds available.

## Storage &amp; tech

**Q: Where are assets stored?**
A: All assets/metadata use the **Net Protocol** (content-addressed). See [Storage](STORAGE.md).

**Q: Are custom contracts used?**
A: Yes — see [Contracts](CONTRACTS.md) for TinyFrensNFT/Art/Sprite and NetIntegratedERC721A.

**Q: What syncs across devices?**
A: Wallets, shown NFTs, size/movement, display name, linked socials. Device credentials stay private.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Saves disappeared?**
A: Saves are device-local. Uninstall/clear data removes local save. Update to latest, relink if needed.

**Q: Need more help?**
A: See [How To](HOW-TO.md), in-app Help, or full [docs](README.md).
