THE PRACTICAL BITS

Short answers.

Can iPhone float over other apps?

iPhone keeps your turtle inside TinyFrens. Our native iOS app is being prepared for an App Store release. Android and desktop support interactive floating overlays.

What happens if I sell my turtle?

Ownership is checked on connection and renewed every 24 hours while you use TinyFrens. Display can continue until that check expires. An expired check needs an internet connection; unlinking removes access immediately.

Where is my pet’s data?

Android stores it on your phone. Browser, iPhone home-screen, and desktop each keep their own local save. Clearing app data can remove it.

How does Bankr know which device is mine?

The app or browser on your chosen device creates a temporary pairing code. Give it to your Bankr agent, which signs a read-only connection message with your wallet. Return to that device and accept the verified wallet and turtle. Your device’s private connection key never goes into chat.

How do updates work?

Use the app’s update prompt or download the latest build from GitHub. After updating, reopen TinyFrens and show your pet again.
