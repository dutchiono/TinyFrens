ANDROID PHONE

Let your pet roam! 🐾

Ask a grown-up to help with these first steps. Then you can play!

  1. Tap Get Android app below.
  2. Grown-up step: Open the downloaded file, tap Install, and follow your phone’s permission message. If it asks, allow this browser to install this app.
  3. Open Pocket Familiar (that’s TinyFrens!). Tap Show pet.
  4. Grown-up step: Turn on Display over other apps for Pocket Familiar. Go back and tap Show pet again.
  5. Your friend is here! Drag to move. Tap or hold for snack 🍓, play 🎾, nap 🌙, or ☰ to open the app.

A home for your fren 🏠

FLOATING VIDEO

Take your fren along 🎬

  1. Open the pet page in Safari on iPhone, or a browser that supports floating video.
  2. Find Take your fren along. Tap Float pet.
  3. If the video is still loading, let it start and tap Float pet again.
  4. Switch apps. If supported, the little video window comes with you!
  5. Come back to feed or play. Tap Stop video when you’re done.
Try video pet ↗

This is an animated video, so care buttons stay in the app. Your phone decides whether it can float. If it won’t float from the home-screen app, try Safari.

My video won’t float

Tap Play on the video first. Try its own Picture in Picture button. On iPhone, ask a grown-up to check Settings → General → Picture in Picture → Start PiP Automatically. Your pet still works here if PiP is unavailable.

HOME-SCREEN WIDGET

A little hello on your home screen.

The native iPhone app includes a small TinyFrens widget that opens your pet when tapped. It is a shortcut companion, not a moving video or a live care display.

Grown-up setup: This needs the signed native iOS app. The free Safari version cannot install this widget. Once the native app is installed and opened, hold an empty spot on your home screen, choose Edit → Add Widget (or +), find TinyFrens, and tap Add Widget.

Android’s floating pet is available now. A native Android home-screen widget is not included yet.

THE PRACTICAL BITS

A few things to know.

Where is my pet’s data?

Android stores it on your phone. The browser and iOS versions each have their own local save. There is no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data can remove your pet.

Can the iPhone pet float over other apps?

Try Float pet in Safari for an animated video window. iOS controls that window; our interactive pet and care buttons stay in the page. Android has the transparent interactive overlay.

Why does my Android pet disappear?

Phone battery restrictions can stop the service. Reopen the app and tap Show pet. After an APK update, reopen the app to show your pet again. Use Hide pet to dismiss it intentionally.

I’m setting up the project. Where are the instructions?

See the GitHub setup guide for release signing and iOS build instructions. This is an early prototype; build status is available in GitHub Actions.
