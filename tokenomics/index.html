01 / ON-CHAIN

FRENS

FRENS is an ERC-20 utility token on Base. TinyFrens is designed to use it for optional economy features such as purchasing additional Energy and, later, staking rewards and capped gameplay boosts.

Those future systems are not promises of profit. Production deposits, staking, and holding or burn boosts are currently inactive. TinyFrens does not require custody of your wallet or private keys.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Energy

Energy belongs to your TinyFrens account. It is not a token, cannot be transferred, and cannot be redeemed for FRENS. An account must own at least one verified Robots v1 NFT to recharge Energy or use Robot-gated game actions.

03 / ACTIONS

What Energy does

Energy keeps play paced without changing the NFT itself. Rechargeable Energy is spent before purchased Energy. Current starting costs are configurable as the game is tuned.

04 / NOT ACTIVE YET

What comes later

Holding, staking, and permanently burning FRENS are being modeled as capped, diminishing-return Energy boosts. Creator-fee sharing and on-chain jackpot infrastructure are also design work—not live production features.

Before any of that turns on, TinyFrens will publish the final rates, contract addresses, caps, risks, and test results. No mainnet deposit or irreversible burn should be inferred from a draft design.
