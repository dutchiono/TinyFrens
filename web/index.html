Android setup

Ask a grown-up to help. Tap Show pet, allow Display over other apps, then come back and tap Show pet again. Drag to move; tap or hold for care bubbles. ☰ opens this app.

iPhone home-screen install

Open tinyfrens.lol in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. For the floating workaround, open in Safari and use Float pet video.

Keep my pet safe

Updating keeps your pet. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data can remove your save. Saves stay on this device.
