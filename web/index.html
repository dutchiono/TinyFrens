How to play

Feed fills their Food. Play makes your fren happy. Nap gives them energy. Clean and give a vitamin when your fren needs it. Wave, dance, or put out a toy just for fun.

Android setup

Ask a grown-up to help. Tap Show pet, allow Display over other apps, then come back and tap Show pet again. Drag to move; tap or hold for care bubbles. ☰ opens this app.

iPhone home-screen install

Open tinyfrens.lol in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Native iPhone support is being prepared for an App Store release. Your NFT stays inside TinyFrens.

Keep my pet safe
