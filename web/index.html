How to play 🐾

🍓 Feed gives a snack. 🎾 Play makes your fren happy. 🌙 Rest gives them energy. You can change their look below!

Android: show my floating pet
  1. Ask a grown-up to help with setup.
  2. Tap Show pet. Allow Display over other apps when your phone asks.
  3. Go back and tap Show pet again.
  4. Drag your fren to move. Tap or hold for snack, play, nap, and ☰ to open this app.

Hide pet sends them home. Free roam lets them wander; Bubble keeps them in place.

iPhone: put my pet on the home screen

Open in Safari. Tap Share (square with an up arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add. Tap the new icon to play.

Updates and help for grown-ups

Keep this app installed to keep your pet. Use Install update or Download update when offered; approve installation, reopen the app, then tap Show pet. Use Allow floating in settings to reach the floating permission. Phone menu names can vary.
