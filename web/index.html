Help

Need a hand?

How to play

Feed fills their Food. Play makes your fren happy. Nap gives them energy. Clean and give a vitamin when your fren needs it. Wave, dance, or put out a toy just for fun. Check Game & RPG docs for details.

Gameplay basics

TinyFrens are tamagotchi-like companions. Care keeps them happy and energetic. Visit other frens, hang in party rooms, and expect more quests/adventures as the game expands.

Where is everything stored?

All TinyFrens assets and metadata are stored using the Net Protocol (content-addressed storage). Token URIs point to Net content for verifiable, portable media. See Storage docs for more.

Android setup

Ask a grown-up to help. Tap Show fren, allow Display over other apps, then come back and tap Show fren again. Drag to move; tap or hold for care bubbles. ☰ opens this app.

iPhone home-screen install

Open tinyfrens.lol in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Native iPhone support is being prepared for an App Store release. Your NFT stays inside TinyFrens.

What happens if I sell my NFT?

Ownership is checked on connection and renewed every 24 hours while you use TinyFrens. Display can continue until that check expires. An expired check needs an internet connection; unlinking removes access immediately.

Keep my fren safe

Updating keeps your fren. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data can remove your save. Saves stay on this device.
