Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains how TinyFrens collects and uses information when you use its websites, apps, games, Telegram and Discord integrations, and related services (the “Services”).

Information we collect

  • Platform identity and activity. When you use a Telegram or Discord integration, we may receive your platform user ID, username, display name, server or group identifiers, channel identifiers, commands, button responses, and message content needed to provide or moderate the requested feature.
  • Account and game data. We store TinyFrens device and account identifiers, profile settings, linked devices, selected companions, game progress, rooms, social connections, and moderation records such as warnings or filters.
  • Wallet and NFT data. If you connect a wallet, we process its public address, signed proofs, and public NFT ownership or metadata. TinyFrens does not ask for or store your wallet seed phrase or private key.
  • Technical and support data. We may process IP addresses, security and rate-limit events, server logs, device or browser information, push-subscription details, and information you send in support or issue reports.
  • On-device data. Some preferences and credentials are stored locally in your browser or app. You can remove them by resetting the app or clearing its local data.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate you, operate and synchronize the Services, run games and shared rooms, provide moderation and safety tools, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, diagnose failures, and improve TinyFrens.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed with infrastructure and service providers, with platforms you choose to use such as Discord, Telegram, and GitHub, or when required for security or law. Blockchain transactions and public wallet activity are visible on public networks. TinyFrens does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed to operate the Services, protect users, keep moderation history, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion, email Tinyfrens@agentmail.to. Some security records may need to be retained, and information written to a public blockchain cannot be changed or deleted by TinyFrens.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards and limit platform access to what features require. No service or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in their country. Do not use the Services if you are not old enough to consent to these terms.

International use and changes

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. We may update this policy as TinyFrens changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: Tinyfrens@agentmail.to.
