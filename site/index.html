THE PRACTICAL BITS

Short answers.

Can iPhone float over other apps?

iPhone keeps your NFTs inside TinyFrens. Our native iOS app is being prepared for an App Store release. Android and desktop support interactive floating overlays.

What happens if I sell my NFT?

Ownership is checked on connection and renewed every 24 hours while you use TinyFrens. Display can continue until that check expires. An expired check needs an internet connection; unlinking removes access immediately.

What syncs with my account?

Linked devices share wallets, shown NFTs, size and movement settings, display name, and linked social identities. Device credentials stay private to each device.

How does Bankr know which device is mine?

TinyFrens creates a temporary pairing code. Give it to Bankr, which signs a read-only connection message with your wallet. Return to that device and approve the wallet. Your private device credential never enters chat.

How do updates work?

Use the app’s update prompt or download the latest build from GitHub. After updating, reopen TinyFrens and show your pet again.
