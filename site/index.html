02 / BRING ONE HOME

FLOATING PET

Android

The full floating companion. Android 8 or newer.

  1. Download the signed APK from Releases.
  2. Open it and approve installation. Allow installs from your browser if asked.
  3. Open Pocket Familiar, tap Show pet, and allow Display over other apps.
  4. Return and tap Show pet again. Drag your fren; tap them to open care.
View Android releases ↗

Signed builds appear here when published.

HOME-SCREEN PET

iPhone / iPad

Start with the web pet today. It lives inside its own window.

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap Try the pet below.
  3. Use Share → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch from your home screen to feed, play and rest.

THE PRACTICAL BITS

A few things to know.

Where is my pet’s data?

Android stores it on your phone. The browser and iOS versions each have their own local save. There is no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data can remove your pet.

Can the iPhone pet float over other apps?

No. This iOS version lives inside its own app or home-screen web window. Android supports the separate floating overlay.

Why does my Android pet disappear?

Phone battery restrictions can stop the service. Reopen the app and tap Show pet. After an APK update, reopen the app to show your pet again. Use Hide pet to dismiss it intentionally.

I’m setting up the project. Where are the instructions?

See the GitHub setup guide for release signing and iOS build instructions. This is an early prototype; build status is available in GitHub Actions.
