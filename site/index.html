THE PRACTICAL BITS

Short answers.

Can iPhone float over other apps?

Not interactively. iPhone can use the home-screen web app, and Safari Picture in Picture can fake a floating video. Android has the real interactive overlay today.

Where is my pet’s data?

Android stores it on your phone. Browser, iPhone home-screen, and desktop each keep their own local save. Clearing app data can remove it.

What is the Bankr wallet?

The Wallet tab uses the server’s Bankr wallet now. Later, a Bankr partner key can give each TinyFren its own wallet.

How do updates work?

Use the app’s update prompt or download the latest build from GitHub. After updating, reopen TinyFrens and show your pet again.
